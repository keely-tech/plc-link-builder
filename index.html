House rules

  • Everything is lowercase with hyphens, never spaces: brief-2026-11, not Brief Nov 2026.
  • Only ever tag links pointing to our own sites. Never tag internal links between pages on the website, because that restarts the visit and hides its real source.
  • Casafari smartlinks (smartlink.casafaricrm.com) can't be tagged and don't show in Google Analytics. Read who opened them in Casafari. When you send a link to the property page on our website instead, tag it, with the property reference as the campaign: pl-1158.
  • Each partner gets a fixed source name, used every time: henley, prestige-radiance, biz-capital.
  • Advisors: always choose your name before copying a link you post or send yourself.
  • Posting into a Facebook group, LinkedIn group or forum thread? Type the group or thread name every time, spelled the same way each time, so the results add up in one place.
  • To see results in Google Analytics, open Explore and add Session manual ad content (shows the advisor) and Session manual term (shows the group or thread).
  • When an enquiry arrives, record the same source in the Casafari lead-source field.
